Privacy policy

Math Crunch Madness. Last updated October 15, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Math Crunch Madness does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the levels you have opened, your stars and best scores, your number size, and the level you are playing) is saved on your device, in the app's own storage. The game never sends it anywhere. Like other app data it can be part of your own iPhone backup, and it is removed when you delete the app.

The internet

The game is played entirely on your device. It does not send anything over the internet.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, this page will say so with a new date. Questions are welcome on the support page.
