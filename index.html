Math Crunch Madness

Tap the tiles. Make it true. Watch it burst.

Math Crunch Madness is a puzzle made of math. Tap tiles anywhere on the board, in the order you would write them, so they read as a true equation, like 9 + 9 = 18, 4 + 5 + 3 − 1 = 11 or 7² = 49, and it crunches in a burst of color. The answers are already on the board, and longer equations pay more.

The game works like a placement exam: every level starts with small numbers, and as you find equations without the hint they grow, from Small to Medium to Big; miss and they ease back. New operations arrive level by level, the levels never end, and the game dresses up for the season.

No ads, no accounts, no data collected. It plays offline.
