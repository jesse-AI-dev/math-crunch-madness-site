Math Crunch Madness

Swap two tiles. Make the equation true. Watch it burst.

Math Crunch Madness is a swap puzzle made of math. Swap two touching tiles so a row or a column reads as a true equation, like 3 + 4 = 7, 7² = 49 or 1/2 = 0.5, and it crunches in a burst of color. Tiles fall, new ones drop in, and chains keep going.

The game works like a placement exam: find moves without the hint and your numbers grow, from 2 × 2 to 22 × 22 to 222 × 222; miss and they ease back. New operations arrive level by level, the levels never end, and the game dresses up for the season.

No ads, no accounts, no data collected. It plays offline.
