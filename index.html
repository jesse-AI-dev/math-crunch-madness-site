Math Crunch Madness

Tap the tiles. Make it true. Watch it burst.

Math Crunch Madness is a puzzle made of math. Tap tiles anywhere on the board, in the order you would write them, so they read as a true equation, like 9 + 9 = 18, 4 + 5 + 3 − 1 = 11 or 7² = 49, and it crunches in a burst of color. The answers are already on the board, and longer equations pay more.

Choose Novice, Intermediate or Hard: harder means bigger numbers, from 9 + 9 = 18 to 24 + 18 = 42 to 45 + 36 = 81. There are twelve worlds, each one kind of math and each a place of its own, with five short levels in each and a real ending. Finish a world and its place lights up on the map, and a sixty-second bonus round opens, where longer equations multiply your points and put time back on the clock. A family can share one device: each player has a game of their own, and a leaderboard ranks them. The game dresses up for the season.

No ads, no accounts, no data collected. It plays offline.
